This page reads nfl.json and cfb.json from its own folder, plus top_picks.json and ai_notes.json when they're there. Browsers don't allow that when the page is opened straight from your computer, so drop the files from your app_data folder here instead.

For fans and research, not betting advice. In testing on past seasons, the model's picks against the spread won about half the time, below the 52.4% needed to profit at standard odds.

AI notes are written by Claude from web searches. They can be wrong or out of date, so check the links. The AI adds notes only; it never changes a pick.

Data: nflverse, sportsdataverse and CollegeFootballData. Current college lines come from ESPN, and player prop odds from The Odds API.
